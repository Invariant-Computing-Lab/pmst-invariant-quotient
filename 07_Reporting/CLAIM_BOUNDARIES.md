# Claim Boundaries

## Supported by this package
- Numerical exactness of selected trace/commutator characteristic invariants under U(3) simultaneous conjugation.
- Completeness of the elementary-symmetric composition fingerprint on the explicitly tested finite alphabet.
- Exact nuisance invariance and canonical reconstructibility of J = cd/b^2 in the declared four-term synthetic law family.
- Separation of charge-sector-preserving and charge-sector-breaking perturbations in the declared finite-dimensional effective Hamiltonian.
- Falsification of the previously stated recursive convex-hull geometry as written.

## Not supported / not claimed
- Physical validity of PMST.
- Quarks or leptons being D0-brane composites.
- Derivation of SU(3) color, Standard Model gauge structure, chirality, anomaly cancellation, CKM/PMNS structure, or observed masses.
- Derivation or physical explanation of exactly three fermion generations.
- A valid compactification manifold, Ricci-flat geometry, C-field/G4 flux solution, or M-theory vacuum.
- Clinical, biological, consciousness, or neurophysical conclusions.
